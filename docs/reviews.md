For any search with local intent — "marketing agency Dublin",

zero you are not competing in that box at all.

Going from 0 to 10 is the single highest-return thing available to this
business right now. It costs nothing, it is entirely within your
control, and unlike backlinks it does not depend on anyone else's
editorial decision.

## Who to ask

Everyone in `config/case-studies.ts` is a real client and a legitimate
ask. That is nine businesses. There are also past clients and anyone
you have done a one-off piece of work for.

Ask each one individually. Never in a group message, never a bulk
mailout — both convert badly and one of them can get reviews filtered.

## What works

- **Ask on a call or in person**, at the moment something has just gone
  well — a good month's numbers, a job landed, a report they were
  pleased with. Then send the link while you are still talking.
- **Send the link immediately**, in the same conversation. A promise to
  send it later is a review you will not get.
- **Ask for something specific.** "Would you mind mentioning the
  valuation campaign?" produces a far more useful review than "would
  you mind leaving a review", and specific reviews rank and convert
  better than generic ones.
- **Space them out.** Ten reviews in one afternoon on a profile with
  none looks exactly like what Google's filters are built to catch.
  Two or three a week is fine.

## What not to do

- Never offer anything in exchange. It violates Google's policy and can
  cost you the profile.
- Never write one yourself or have someone write one who was not a
  customer.
- Do not use a review-gating tool that only sends happy customers to
  Google. Also against policy.

## A message you can send

Keep it short and make it easy to say no to.

> Hi [name] — quick one. We are trying to build up our Google reviews
> and yours would carry a lot of weight. If you have two minutes:
> [link]
>
> If you can mention [the specific thing] that would be ideal. No
> problem at all if you would rather not.

## Reply to every one

Including any bad one, briefly and without arguing. The reply is read
by the next prospect far more than by the reviewer.

## One profile setting worth checking

The Maps listing places the business near the centre of the country
rather than in Dublin, which is what happens when a service-area
business covers everywhere. If the service area is set to all 26
counties, consider narrowing it to where you genuinely want to rank —
Dublin and the commuter belt. A broad service area does not help you
rank in far counties, and it can dilute relevance close to home.
