## The families, and which ones earn their place

Assigned from Search Console evidence rather than a generic template.

| family | example | evidence | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|

| `<niche> <county>` | garage conversion dublin | 100% CTR on first impression | **build** |
| `<niche> marketing` | wedding venue marketing | 12.3 | **build** |
| `<niche> SEO` | roofing seo ireland | 62 pages, 88 imp, position **81** | **stop building** |
| `lead generation <x>` | lead generation ireland | position **28-83** | avoid the phrasing |
| `marketing agency <county>` | marketing agency cork | position **50-99** | not winnable from Dublin |

The two 'stop' rows matter more than the three 'build' rows. The SEO
page type has 62 live pages and produces 88 impressions at position
81 with no clicks. Sixty-nine niches have no SEO page and should
stay that way until something changes.

6. <niche> google ads            a guide
