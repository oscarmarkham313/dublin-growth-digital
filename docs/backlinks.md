# Backlinks — the ceiling on everything else

Checked 29 Sep 2026.

## Where you stand

Searching `"dublingrowthdigital.com" -site:dublingrowthdigital.com`
returns social posts and nothing else:

- Instagram captions (several, including reposts by other accounts)
- The Facebook page
- Oscar's LinkedIn profile

Every one of those is `nofollow` and passes no authority. There are no
directory listings, no client credits, no press, no guest posts.

**Effectively zero backlinks.** That is the ceiling on the whole site.
473 pages of genuinely good content on a domain nothing links to will
keep ranking roughly where it ranks now, because Google has no external
evidence that anyone finds this site useful.

Nothing in this file is clever or growth-hacky. It is the ordinary list,
in the order worth doing it.

---

## Tier 1 — you already control these

### Client site credits

You build and run websites for clients. A credit in the footer —
"Website by Dublin Growth Digital" linking back — is a real,
topically relevant, permanent link from an Irish business site.

This is the best link source available to you and it requires nobody's
permission but your clients'. Most will say yes if asked when the site
goes live; some existing ones will say yes if asked now.

Two cautions:
- Ask rather than assume. A credit added without asking sours the
  relationship if they notice it later.
- Do not put the same anchor text on every one. Vary it — the company
  name on most, occasionally nothing but the logo.

**This is your single highest-value action in this file.**

### Your own other properties

Anything you own that already ranks — other sites, landing pages,
profiles — can link here legitimately.

## Tier 2 — free, quick, real

These are citations as much as links. Several are `nofollow`, but
consistent name/address/phone across them is a local ranking signal in
its own right, and the map pack reads them.

| where | notes |
|---|---|
| Bing Places | Free. Mirrors your Google profile. Ten minutes. |
| Apple Business Connect | Free. Feeds Apple Maps and Siri. |
| goldenpages.ie | The Irish directory people still actually use. Free basic listing. |
| Trustpilot | Free tier. Doubles as a second review platform. |
| LinkedIn company page | Separate from your personal profile. You do not appear to have one. |
| Crunchbase | Free listing, decent authority. |

Do these in one sitting. Use exactly the same business name, phone and
website on every one — inconsistency is worse than absence.

## Tier 3 — agency directories

These exist to list agencies and they carry real authority. Most have a
free tier; the paid tiers are generally not worth it at your size.

- **Clutch.co** — the main one. Free profile. Reviews there are
  verified by phone, which makes them valuable and slow to get.
- **Sortlist** — free listing, European focus.
- **DesignRush** — free tier exists.
- **The Manifest** — Clutch's sister site.

Worth doing but do not expect traffic. These are authority and
citations, not a lead source.

---

## Tier 4 — Irish, local, and worth the effort

This is where the genuinely good links are, and they take real work.

- **Local Enterprise Office** — LEOs list client businesses and run
  events, mentoring and training. If you have used any LEO service you
  may already qualify for a listing. If not, their programmes are worth
  it on their own merits and the link follows.
- **Chamber of Commerce** — Dublin Chamber and the county chambers list
  members. Paid membership, so only worth it if you would use it.
- **Irish business press and blogs** — a genuine, specific, data-backed
  pitch gets picked up. You have something most agencies do not: real
  numbers from real Irish campaigns, and a sharp view on things like
  shared lead portals.
- **Podcast appearances** — Irish business and trades podcasts almost
  always link the guest. Low competition for a guest slot, permanent
  link, and it reaches the exact audience you sell to.
- **Supplier and partner pages** — anyone whose tool or service you use
  and would recommend. Many have a partners or customers page.

---

## What not to do

- Do not buy links. It is the fastest way to a manual penalty and the
  sites selling them to Irish SMEs are uniformly bad.
- Do not do bulk directory submissions. Two hundred listings on
  directories nobody visits is a recognisable spam pattern.
- Do not do reciprocal link swaps with other agencies at scale.
- Do not use private blog networks, whatever they are called this year.

Ten real links beat a thousand bought ones, and the bought ones can
take the site down with them.

---

## Realistic expectation

A handful of genuine links will not put you first for "marketing agency
Dublin" — that is a ten-year-old-agency query. What they will do is
raise the floor under the whole domain, which is what lifts the niche
pages from position 20-30 into the range where they get clicked.

The niche and service queries are where you can win. Backlinks are what
let you win them.

---

## Suggested order

1. Client site credits — ask three clients this week
2. The Tier 2 list in one sitting
3. Clutch and Sortlist
4. One podcast pitch a month
5. Chamber or LEO if you would use the membership anyway

Track what you have done. `Ahrefs` and `Semrush` both have free
backlink checkers that will show new links appearing.
