## Before you start: one decision

Your socials are **@dublinsocialagency**. Your business is **Dublin
Growth Digital**. Google uses consistency of name, phone and website
across the web to decide that all these listings are the same business.
A mismatched handle weakens that.

You have two options and either is fine, but pick one before you create
six new profiles:

1. Rename the Facebook and Instagram handles to match the business.
   Cleanest, and the handles appear to be available.
2. Keep them and accept the socials sit slightly apart from the brand.

Do not create new directory listings under a third variation.

## Client footer credits — the best source you have

This is the highest-value item in the whole file and it is the one
nobody but you can do. These are real, permanent, topically relevant
links from Irish business sites.

**Checked 1 October 2026 — none of these currently link to you:**

- dgd-funnel.vercel.app (your own)
- riva-tan.vercel.app (your own)
- brendancocoman.vercel.app
- oneclickgo.ie

Two cautions before you act:

- **Ask the client, do not just add it.** A credit discovered later
  sours a relationship that is worth far more than a link.
- **Vary the anchor text.** Not "Dublin marketing agency" on every one.
  Mostly the company name, sometimes just the logo. Identical anchors
  across a handful of sites is a recognisable pattern.
- Your own properties count for less than a client's would, because
  they are obviously connected. Worth doing, not worth leading with.

### The ask, for a client

> Hi [name] — small thing. We usually put a short credit in the footer
> of sites we build, just "Website by Dublin Growth Digital" linking
> back to us. Completely happy if you would rather not, but it helps us
> a lot and most clients are fine with it. Would you mind?

### The snippet

```html
<p>Website by
  <a href="https://dublingrowthdigital.com" rel="noopener">
    Dublin Growth Digital
  </a>
</p>
```

Do not add `rel="nofollow"` — that would defeat the purpose. Do not add
`rel="sponsored"` either; this is a credit, not a paid placement.

## What to avoid

- Buying links. Fastest route to a manual penalty, and the sites
  selling to Irish SMEs are uniformly bad.
- Bulk directory submissions. Two hundred listings on sites nobody
  visits is a recognisable spam pattern.
- Reciprocal swaps with other agencies at scale.
- Anything described as a private blog network.

Ten real links beat a thousand bought ones, and the bought ones can
take the domain down with them.

### Where you currently surface

On Bing, which is what ChatGPT Search retrieves from:

- `"dublin growth digital"` -- the site ranks, along with the Facebook
  and Instagram profiles. The competitor growthdigital.ie also ranks
  for your name.
- `lead generation agency ireland trades` -- the site appears, roughly
  fifth
- `marketing agency for roofers ireland` -- the site does **not**
  appear. The page-one results include marketingfortrades.ie, a direct
  niche-for-niche competitor, plus constructionmarketing.ie, tilt.ie,
  lmh.agency and brand-depot.com.

### One thing worth knowing about the phrasing

Bing reads "roofing leads" as lead *flashing* -- the metal -- and
returns builders' merchants. The term is ambiguous to a machine in a
way it is not to a roofer. Where it matters, "roofing lead generation"
or "leads for roofing contractors" is less ambiguous, even though
"<niche> leads" is the stronger term on Google.
