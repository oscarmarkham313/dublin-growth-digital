What we do

Two sides to the business: building and maintaining gardens, and the tree work most landscapers will not touch.

Tree surgery

Crown reductions, pruning and full tree removal, with the timber chipped and taken away.

Paving and kerbing

Patios, paths, driveways and kerbing laid on proper foundations so they hold their level.

Lawns and seeding

New lawns seeded or turfed, and tired lawns brought back with feeding, aeration and verti-drain.

Garden maintenance

Regular cutting, pruning, fertilising and weed control — one-off tidy-ups or an ongoing contract.

Drainage

Verti-drain and drainage work for ground that holds water and lawns that never quite dry out.

Commercial grounds

Grounds maintenance for businesses, estates and community spaces across West Cork and South Kerry.
