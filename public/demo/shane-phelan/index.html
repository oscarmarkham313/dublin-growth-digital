What we do

Domestic and commercial landscaping, start to finish. If it is outside the house, we will price it.

Full garden builds

New-build gardens taken from bare ground to finished space — levels, drainage, hard landscaping, planting and lawn.

Paving and patios

Patios, paths and kerbing laid on proper foundations so they stay flat and stay put through Irish winters.

Planting and lawns

Planting schemes chosen for the site and the soil, plus seeded and turfed lawns that establish properly.

Garden maintenance

Ongoing upkeep for gardens we have built and gardens we have not — cutting, pruning, feeding and seasonal tidy-ups.

Fencing and boundaries

Fencing, screening and boundary work built to last, finished to the same standard as the rest of the garden.

Commercial grounds

Grounds and landscaping for businesses, estates and developments across the south east.
